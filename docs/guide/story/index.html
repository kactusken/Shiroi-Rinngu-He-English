How the Story Works

The story is a calendar. Scenes play on fixed dates: on the first of a month (as the month before ends, before you set the new schedule), on a match day before the match, or on the day after a match. Matches are booked on fixed dates too, and you see the card in the scene before.

  • Four choices in the whole game, each a two-item menu: whose attendant you become (April 1995), Onita's offer (May 1996, Kurenai route), the old fisherman (June 1996, Kurenai route) and Michinoku Pro (April 1997, FMW route).
  • Match results: most matches change nothing, or only the line someone says afterwards. The few that steer the story are marked changes the story below.
  • No stat is ever checked. Your stats matter only because they win matches.
  • Your training partners change with the route: each change is noted in the month it happens.

Each match box shows the date, the arena and the card. Where a match has a result scene, Win and Lose say what follows.

The Trainee YearApril 1994 - March 1995 · every route

Your first year at the LLPW dojo is training only: no matches, and nothing you do changes the story. Your partners at the start are Carol Midori, Miki Handa, Mikiko Futagami, Michiko Omukai, Harley Saito and Keiko Aono.

1994

April 1994

April 1. President Rumi Kazama gives the six new trainees their first-morning speech: from today they live as wrestlers, and should reach for a star. The rookies introduce themselves; Kotori Kagono from Osaka boasts she won't lose even to Shinobu Kandori. Coach Harley Saito explains training: train with the seniors and steal their technique, watch your HP, and set the whole month's schedule before you start.

April 8. Naomi Iwashita hasn't come for a week. Kazama writes her off; Mikiko Futagami sneers at the dropout.

May 1994

Start of May. Nagi Akizuki has run away in the night, leaving a note: "Nagi's quiiitting!"

June 1994

No scene.

July 1994

Start of July. Kazama: train with matches in mind. Skills decide how many moves you can use.

August 1994

Start of August. Harley explains the five wrestler types: Strong, Entertainment, Babyface, Power and Heel. Training with someone makes you more like her.

August 12. On lock-up duty during Futagami's late training, you catch Kayo Nakamura sneaking out. She is quitting, she wants a normal life, and she asks you not to give up. Futagami saw it all: "From the day we stood in that ring, we've been wrestlers."

September 1994

Start of September. Kazama takes the rookies to the FMW dojo to plan an interpromotional show, and Atsushi Onita greets them. Yuka Shimamura teases Kagono for dropping her Osaka accent: Kagono is a huge Onita fan.

October - November 1994

No scenes.

December 1994

December 24. Kagono moans about lifting barbells on Christmas Eve, Futagami hints that anyone single is in the same boat, and Miki Handa explodes and sends everyone back to training.

1995

January 1995

Start of January. A New Year greeting: your debuts are coming. Kagono asks to speak to Kazama alone.

January 5. Kagono comes to say goodbye: she and Futagami are moving to Kandori's new promotion, the JWWF. She makes you promise not to quit: "The day you make the main event, we fight." Mikiko Futagami leaves your partner list. See the partners

February 1995

Start of February. Kazama announces the two departures. Eagle Sawai blames Harley's regular army and the two nearly come to blows; Kazama ends it: "We don't chase those who leave."

February 25. The pro test is at the start of March. Train hard, but don't get hurt.

March 1995

Start of March. The pro test. Of the six rookies, two remain.

March 7. You and Shimamura both pass: you are pro wrestlers.

The route choice

As March ends. New pros serve a senior as her attendant. The two without one are Carol Midori of the regular army and Kurenai Yasha of Eagle's army, and Shimamura lets you choose first.

  • Carol You become Carol's attendant: "I won't have you losing to the likes of Shimamura, Kurenai's attendant." Go to the Carol Midori route. Go there
  • Kurenai You become Kurenai's attendant ("You'd better pull your weight") and Shimamura goes to Carol. Go to the Kurenai Yasha route. Go there

The Carol Midori RouteApril 1995 - March 31, 2000

You are Carol Midori's attendant, in Harley Saito's regular army. After a month of joint training in May 1995 your partners for the rest of the game are Carol Midori, Harley Saito, Miki Handa, Michiko Omukai and Keiko Aono.

Warning

On this route you must beat Kotori Kagono on September 14, 1999. In the original Japanese game a loss there stops the story for good: no final and no ending. The English patch fixes this, and a loss now plays Kagono's own scene and goes on.

The Final and the Endings

Every route ends on March 31, 2000 at the Tokyo Dome: you against Shinobu Kandori in a singles match. Her moves are mostly holds on the right arm, right leg and head, with a Tiger Driver late in the match, so Guts and your arm, leg and head HP matter here (see Strategy).

After the match comes the ending, the same on every route: "The fight ended amid huge cheers. Win or lose, I didn't care back then. 'Maybe I grasped the star.'" Then the staff roll, and one of four epilogues, chosen by your route and by whether you won:

You tell how the JWWF and LLPW became one promotion again and Kandori faded away; you won the title, but something was missing. "I'll just keep climbing. Until I grasp a star...
