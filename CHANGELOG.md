# Changelog

## v1.0 (2026-11-12)
- First release: the complete English translation - every story scene on all three routes (Carol Midori,
  Kurenai Yasha, FMW), menus, status and training screens, move names, match announcements, endings and the
  opening title cards.
- Variable-width text renderer and proportional font; the script moved into an expanded 2 MB ROM.
- Fix: losing to Kotori Kagono in September 1999 on the Carol route no longer ends the story.
- The developers' unused training animation viewer is reachable: hold Down+L at power-on.
- Player's guide and story walkthrough: https://kactusken.github.io/Shiroi-Rinngu-He-English/guide/
